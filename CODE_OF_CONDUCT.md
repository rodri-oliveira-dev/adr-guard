# Code of Conduct

ADR Guard welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives. We aim to maintain a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in ADR Guard a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other personal characteristics.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and other project interactions.
- Offer actionable, evidence-based feedback. Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Assume good faith where reasonable, remain open to correction, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Welcome newcomers and help others find relevant documentation or existing discussions.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, and the time and availability of volunteer maintainers and contributors.
- Keep conversations on topic, disclose conflicts of interest when relevant, and avoid intentionally misleading claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, stalking, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory remarks, slurs, or demeaning comments targeting personal characteristics.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, deliberate humiliation, or sustained hostile disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission (doxxing).
- Sharing confidential source code, private Architecture Decision Records, credentials, or other sensitive material without authorization.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in a review.

A strong technical opinion, respectfully expressed and supported by reasoning, is not a violation. Repeatedly disregarding requests to stop harmful behavior may be.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to ADR Guard's GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed communication spaces. It also applies when someone explicitly represents the project in a public community setting.

Project maintainers may edit or hide inappropriate content, request changes in behavior, lock threads, reject contributions, or restrict participation to protect the community.

## Reporting concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that may violate this policy:

1. **Do not publish personal details or sensitive evidence in a public issue or discussion.**
2. Where available, use GitHub's **Report content** feature on the relevant comment, account, or conversation.
3. For project-specific concerns, visit the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/rodri-oliveira-dev) to identify an available contact option and request a **private reporting channel**. Do not include sensitive details in a public contact request.
4. Once a private channel is established, provide a concise account of the incident, relevant URLs, dates, and any evidence you are comfortable sharing.

There is currently no dedicated private conduct-reporting address published in this repository. The GitHub reporting option is available independently of the project maintainers. If a concern involves the maintainer or cannot be reported safely to them, use GitHub's reporting and safety mechanisms rather than raising it publicly.

For software security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead. A conduct report is not a security advisory.

## Handling and enforcement

Maintainers will assess reported behavior in context and seek to respond fairly and proportionately. Possible actions include:

- A private reminder of community expectations or a request to revise content.
- A formal warning or temporary restrictions on participation.
- Removal of content, closure or locking of a discussion, or rejection of a contribution.
- A longer-term participation restriction for repeated or serious misconduct.
- Escalation to GitHub when platform rules or safety concerns warrant it.

The severity, recurrence, potential harm, and willingness to correct behavior will inform the response. Reports should be handled with discretion, sharing details only when needed to assess and address the concern. Complete confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when action or platform escalation requires limited disclosure.

Good-faith reports will not be penalized, and retaliatory behavior is prohibited. People involved may request a reconsideration through an available private channel; the reviewer should consider additional context where feasible.
